How I work

Five steps I run on every design and video project, from the first call to the final export.

01

Listen

Brief, stakeholder interviews and an audit of every place the brand shows up today.

02

Explore

Fast sketches, moodboards and type and color studies, narrowed to two or three clear routes.

03

Design and edit

Layouts, type and color for design work; a rough cut, then a fine cut for video.

04

Polish

Color grading, sound, motion details, and checks for print and every platform.

05

Hand off

Final files in every size and format, source files, and a quick walkthrough.

About

I turn big, messy briefs into designs and videos that teams can actually use.

I started out designing posters and editing videos for friends, which taught me to care about both the frame and the cut. Since then I've worked on brand identities, campaign films and social content for airlines, banks, food brands and tech companies.

I do my best work inside teams: marketers who know the numbers, writers who know the voice, and producers who keep the shoot on schedule.
